PropulsionLab

Rocket engine simulator by SkyLakes Aerospace

About the model

Ideal-rocket model with CEA-derived combustion properties (interpolated), isentropic frozen nozzle with efficiency factors, lumped feed-system lag per cycle, and Saint-Robert burn law r = aPcⁿ with quasi-steady grain burnback.

Presets are checked against published figures to within 5–10%. Two-phase flow, erosive burning, film cooling and real turbomachinery maps are not modelled. It's built for learning, not for certifying hardware.

Free and open, from SkyLakes Aerospace.
